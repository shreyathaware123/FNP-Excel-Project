# 💹 FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard (Excel Project)

This project presents a comprehensive sales performance analysis for FNP (Ferns N Petals) using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard provides insights into revenue trends, customer spending behavior, product performance, city-wise demand, and time-based order analysis.

##  ➡️ Project Objective

To analyze sales data and uncover insights related to:

* Revenue growth patterns
* Customer purchasing behavior
* Occasion-based demand trends
* Product performance
* City-wise sales distribution
* Delivery efficiency

The goal is to enable data-driven decision-making for marketing strategy, inventory planning, and revenue optimization.

## ➡️ Key KPIs Analyzed

* Total Orders: 1000
* Total Revenue: ₹35,20,984
* Average Order Delivery Time: 5.53 days
* Average Customer Spending: ₹3,520.98

## 💹 Dashboard Insights

### 1️⃣ Revenue by Occasion

* Highest revenue generated during Anniversary and Raksha Bandhan
* Lower performance during Diwali and Valentine’s (relative comparison)
* Occasion-driven sales highlight seasonal marketing opportunities

### 2️⃣ Revenue by Category (Day-Wise Trends)

* Strong sales performance on Sundays and Tuesdays
* Lower engagement on mid-week days
* Helps optimize promotional timing

### 3️⃣ Revenue by Month

* Revenue peaks observed during February and August
* Seasonal spikes align with gifting occasions
* Useful for demand forecasting and campaign planning

### 4️⃣ Top 5 Products by Revenue

* Identified highest revenue-generating products
* Helps prioritize inventory and promotions

### 5️⃣ Top 10 Cities by Orders

* Dhanbad and Imphal among top-performing cities
* Regional concentration of demand visible
* Supports location-based marketing strategies

### 6️⃣ Revenue by Order Time (Hourly Analysis)

* Higher revenue during evening hours
* Indicates customer preference for post-work purchases
* Useful for ad scheduling and operational planning

## 💡 Business Impact

This dashboard helps businesses:

* Identify high-performing occasions and products
* Optimize marketing campaigns by season & time
* Improve delivery efficiency tracking
* Enhance city-wise sales targeting
* Increase revenue through data-backed decisions

The analysis reveals that sales are highly occasion-driven and time-sensitive, highlighting the importance of targeted promotions and operational efficiency.
